SHIFTNAKE Android Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026 (from publication)

  • App: SHIFTNAKE Android (com.sleeplesshan.shiftnake)
  • Applies to: the adult-advertising configuration of Android release 1.0.0/code14
  • Operator: Hanmin Kim, an individual developer based in South Korea
  • Privacy contact: sleeplesshan@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy describes game saves, advertising and privacy choices, and support inquiries for SHIFTNAKE Android, provided by Hanmin Kim. The game is for a mixed-age audience including children, while advertising is configured only for users who explicitly select age 18 or older. Users selecting a younger age or choosing not to disclose their age can still play ordinary games.

Visits to the support website and support email processing are explained separately in the website and inquiry privacy notice. This document concerns the Android app and is distinct from the website notice.

2. On-device game saves

The following information is stored in the device’s app storage to provide gameplay and maintain progress:

  • In-game currency, progress and best records, unlocks and owned/equipped skins
  • Selected game mode, music, sound effects, volumes, track and language
  • One-handed input, rendering quality and other display/control settings
  • Local records needed for deaths, continuing a run and ad timing

Current native persistence uses app-private SQLite and other on-device storage paths. There is no developer-operated game account, login or remote save server, and no implemented feature uploading these game saves to a developer server. Google SDK communications and operating-system backup are separate paths.

The current Android configuration permits system backup. Depending on the device, operating-system settings and backup service, app data may be backed up or transferred to and restored on another device. This does not mean that the app provides its own account-based cloud-save feature, nor guarantee that no information ever leaves the device.

3. Age selection and information not entered directly

The app does not require registration or entry of a name, phone number, government ID number or exact date of birth. Features requiring contacts, camera, microphone or precise location are not currently implemented.

An age range can be selected in ad-preparation flows, such as the first game start, and in related settings. Current options are under 13, ages 13–17, 18 or older, and no selection. This is not identity or actual-age verification. The app does not store a date of birth or preselect the adult option.

The selected range is currently held in the app code’s session memory, not as a permanent game-save key or user profile field. SDK-managed consent state is separate, so closing the app does not mean that all personal information or consent state is deleted.

4. Google advertising and consent SDKs and communications

The current build includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. No separate Firebase Analytics or third-party mediation SDK has been added. The production build uses registered AdMob rewarded and interstitial ad units. After the adult-advertising gate is satisfied, ads may be requested in advance during play so they are ready to display. Rewarded ads may be shown when you choose to watch for an ad-rewarded continue, and interstitial ads when the in-game display conditions are met. Availability depends on age treatment, privacy choices, connectivity, Google ad serving and app approval, among other factors. Non-personalized ads do not mean that the processing described below is absent.

The app blocks automatic ad-SDK initialization and starts ad-consent checks and ad initialization/loading only after an explicit age-18-or-older choice. The under-13, ages-13–17 and no-selection paths do not make those advertising or consent-information requests. UMP consent-information requests and preparation/display of a required form may communicate before an ad is loaded. The app code checks UMP’s permission to request ads and SDK readiness before requesting an ad and does not bypass an error or closed gate. This is not an enumeration of every internal SDK or operating-system action, or a guarantee of no communications before a particular point.

Google discloses that its advertising SDK automatically collects and shares the following data categories for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This app applies the age gate and removed permissions described above, along with feature and privacy-choice settings:

  • IP address and approximate location that may be inferred from it
  • App launches, taps, ad/video views and other usage/interactions
  • App/SDK performance, launch time, hangs, energy usage and diagnostics
  • Available device-, app- or account-related identifiers, such as App Set ID where available under the system and settings
  • Related information needed to check consent and provide messages and privacy-choice management

The manifest removes the Android advertising-ID permission and certain AdServices permissions. This does not mean that all identifying information, including IP or app-related identifiers, is absent. Google describes TLS protection for advertising-SDK data in transit; this is not a guarantee that all stored data is independently encrypted or absolutely secure.

Ordinary online-status checking reads Android network-connectivity and validation state. The app’s network adapter does not implement separate HTTP connectivity probes or upload a connection history to a developer server. Operating-system and external-SDK communications are separate.

5. Age-based advertising and privacy choices

Ads and ad-rewarded continues are not provided for under 13, ages 13–17 or no selection. Ordinary gameplay, including starting a new round, remains available. An absent age choice is not treated as an adult choice.

Even after an explicit 18-or-older choice, every ad request uses the non-personalized setting (npa=1) and maximum ad content rating G. These settings do not mean that no personal information is collected, and they are not the same as a sale/sharing opt-out signal. Actual ad availability may be limited by Google’s status, privacy choices and other conditions.

An age choice is not identity, actual-age or parent/guardian-consent verification. The app does not claim to provide a separate guardian-verification function and does not assume a parent has consented to advertising for a child. Support requests needing personal information about a user under 14 should come through a parent or guardian. Do not send unnecessary child names, exact birth dates, photos or locations. If unnecessary child information is identified, minimal handling and prompt minimization/deletion take priority.

App settings provide initial age selection and a retry with the current choice. The ordinary production settings do not provide repeated changes of age within the same running session. In the adult-advertising path, when UMP reports that privacy options are required and a form is available, “Privacy options” in settings opens the relevant form. Availability depends on region, SDK status, message availability and other conditions.

Separate Korean and English privacy-policy links open in an external browser without requiring an advertising-consent choice. An ad/consent-check failure is not intended to indefinitely block an ordinary online new game. Changing a consent choice does not delete all information already held by Google or partners; deletion and rights requests may involve separate procedures.

6. External processing parties and advertising partners

Google advertising/consent services and the advertising-technology partners that actually apply may process information under their service terms and privacy policies. Google’s public terms for a South Korean billing country identify Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. as the contracting entity; Google’s privacy policy describes services of Google LLC and relevant affiliates. A contracting entity, its processing role and actual storage locations are not assumed to be the same thing. Google’s service-specific data-protection information describes AdMob advertising within its independent-controller services, while separately identified processor functions depend on the services used. Google is not represented as processing all app information solely on the operator’s instructions.

No third-party mediation SDK has been added to the current production build. This does not guarantee that no other Google service providers or advertising-related parties ever process information. Where a consent message is provided, its partner information and those partners’ privacy policies can also be consulted.

Partner configuration may change with account settings and service scope, including automatic updates. A selected account list or a complete reference directory is not proof that every listed party actually received information. A non-personalized ad request is not the same as a sale/sharing opt-out signal or a stop to all external processing. Where the relevant US privacy-choice screen is provided, you can save an opt-out choice there.

7. International processing

Google explains that information may be processed on servers worldwide. SDK communications, service provision or operating-system backup services may therefore involve processing outside South Korea. The country of a contracting company is not an exhaustive statement of the actual server countries or routes.

Advertising information is processed through Google’s SDK in the adult-advertising flow described above. There is no feature that collects game saves on a developer server and then transfers them in bulk to Google. Consult Google’s information below for its international processing, retention and rights procedures as well. This policy does not identify the actual server country or route of every ad request, or state that one contract or consent mechanism covers every international operation. The location of an advertising-contract entity may differ from the actual processing locations.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device game saves are retained for gameplay and maintaining progress/settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling through Android may delete them and cause loss of progress, currency and settings. Menus and backup behavior vary by device; OS backup/transfer data and external-service data are not guaranteed to be deleted simultaneously.

Google SDK/service retention varies by information type, purpose, account/service settings, security/fraud prevention and legal obligations. Some information is managed through deletion requests or account controls; some expires or is anonymized after specified periods, or is kept longer for limited reasons. There is no single fixed retention period asserted for all SDK data. Ad-report retention is not presented as the retention period of individual SDK-collected information.

Support messages and attachments held by the operator are deleted within 90 days after inquiry completion. Ongoing inquiries and legally required records are assessed individually, and unnecessary/sensitive information is minimized earlier. Independent security, backup or other processing by Google/GitHub is not guaranteed to be erased on the same deadline.

9. Rights and safeguards

You or a legally authorized guardian may send access, correction, deletion, suspension, consent-withdrawal or other requests under applicable law to the support email. Only minimum necessary verification will be requested. Do not send unsolicited passwords, verification codes or unnecessary identity-document copies.

The developer does not operate an administrative server that remotely reads or deletes on-device game saves. You may need to manage device-held information in device settings. Provider procedures also apply to information held directly by external providers. Uninstalling, changing consent or changing one Google account setting does not guarantee immediate deletion of every related record.

The app uses Android app-storage access restrictions. Save checksums detect corruption; they are not personal-data encryption. Access is limited to what is needed and unnecessary inquiry copies are minimized, but absolute security is not guaranteed.

10. Access and updates

Korean and English app-privacy-policy links in settings open this policy in an external browser, independently of advertising-consent choices. The UMP privacy-options control is different from a general policy link and is shown when Google reports that it is required for the current status.

If features, SDKs, purposes, partners, audience or retention criteria change, the policy and effective date are updated and separate notice/consent procedures are used where legally required. For rights concerning information already held, use the support contact above and the relevant provider procedures.
