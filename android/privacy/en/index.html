SHIFTNAKE Android Privacy Policy

Development and private testing · Effective: October 20, 2026 (from publication)

  • App: SHIFTNAKE Android (com.sleeplesshan.shiftnake)
  • Current basis: development build 0.1.9/code10, Google test ads
  • Operator: Hanmin Kim, an individual developer based in South Korea
  • Privacy contact: sleeplesshan@gmail.com

1. Scope

This policy applies to SHIFTNAKE Android development and private testing directly managed by adult developers and adult reviewers. This stage does not include recruiting actual child testers, public APK distribution or production monetized advertising. Adult reviewers may select minor or unknown-age options to test protection behavior.

This does not redefine the game as adult-only or remove the planned child-inclusive mixed-age development direction. Actual store target ages and content ratings are determined separately. The restriction is managed through private-test distribution and operations, not identity or age verification. Before a public service involving children or an expansion of processing, the necessary processing basis, notices, consent verification and ad review must be completed and this policy updated.

Visits to the support website and support email processing are explained separately in the website and inquiry privacy notice. This document concerns the Android app and is distinct from the website notice.

2. On-device game saves

The following information is stored in the device’s app storage to provide gameplay and maintain progress:

  • In-game currency, progress and best records, unlocks and owned/equipped skins
  • Selected game mode, music, sound effects, volumes, track and language
  • One-handed input, rendering quality and other display/control settings
  • Local records needed for deaths, continuing a run and ad timing

Current native persistence uses app-private SQLite and other on-device storage paths. There is no developer-operated game account, login or remote save server, and no implemented feature uploading these game saves to a developer server. Google SDK communications and operating-system backup are separate paths.

The current Android configuration permits system backup. Depending on the device, operating-system settings and backup service, app data may be backed up or transferred to and restored on another device. This does not mean that the app provides its own account-based cloud-save feature, nor guarantee that no information ever leaves the device.

3. Age selection and information not entered directly

The app does not require registration or entry of a name, phone number, government ID number or exact date of birth. Features requiring contacts, camera, microphone or precise location are not currently implemented.

An age range can be selected in ad-preparation flows and related settings. Current options are under 13, ages 13–17, 18 or older, and no selection. This is not identity or actual-age verification. The app does not store a date of birth or preselect the adult option.

The selected range is currently held in the app code’s session memory, not as a permanent game-save key or user profile field. SDK-managed consent state is separate, so closing the app does not mean that all personal information or consent state is deleted.

4. Google advertising and consent SDKs and communications

The current build includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. No separate Firebase Analytics or third-party mediation SDK has been added. It uses only Google sample rewarded/interstitial ad units, not production monetized ad units. Test ads do not mean that no information is processed.

The app code’s consent configuration/update and ad initialization/loading flow starts when ad preparation is needed. UMP consent-information requests and preparation/display of a required form may communicate before an ad is loaded. The app code checks UMP’s permission to request ads and SDK readiness before requesting an ad and does not bypass an error or closed gate. This is not an enumeration of every internal SDK or operating-system action, or a guarantee of no communications before a particular point.

Google’s official SDK disclosure describes the following categories as potentially collected and shared, depending on settings, permissions, features and consent, for advertising, usage/performance analysis, diagnostics, fraud prevention and service provision:

  • IP address and approximate location that may be inferred from it
  • App launches, taps, ad/video views and other usage/interactions
  • App/SDK performance, launch time, hangs, energy usage and diagnostics
  • Available device-, app- or account-related identifiers, such as App Set ID where available under the system and settings
  • Related information needed to check consent and provide messages and privacy-choice management

The manifest removes the Android advertising-ID permission and certain AdServices permissions. This does not mean that all identifying information, including IP or app-related identifiers, is absent. Google describes TLS protection for advertising-SDK data in transit; this is not a guarantee that all stored data is independently encrypted or absolutely secure.

Ordinary online-status checking reads Android network-connectivity and validation state. The app’s network adapter does not implement separate HTTP connectivity probes or upload a connection history to a developer server. Operating-system and external-SDK communications are separate.

5. Advertising protections and consent choices

For under 13, ages 13–17 and no selection, the app currently applies conservative CHILD age treatment and UMP’s under-age-of-consent signal. The 18-or-older selection uses different age signals, but all ad requests are implemented with a non-personalized setting (npa=1) and maximum ad content rating G.

These settings do not replace national legal age definitions, actual-age verification or parent/legal guardian consent verification. They are not certification that every ad or every SDK process meets all legal obligations. UMP manages user privacy choices; it is not a separate legal-guardian verification procedure. The app does not represent that such a separate guardian-verification process is implemented.

Age selection can be revisited from app settings. When UMP reports that privacy options are required and can provide the form, the app is implemented to open that form from “Advertising privacy → Privacy options.” Actual availability depends on region, SDK state, message availability and other conditions. Configuration and screen behavior remain part of development/private-test verification, not completed production-service verification.

Ads may be limited or unavailable depending on choices or communications. The app is implemented so that an ad/consent-check failure does not indefinitely block an ordinary online new game. Changing a consent choice does not erase all information previously held by Google or partners; deletion and rights requests may require separate procedures.

6. External processing parties and advertising partners

Google advertising/consent services and the advertising-technology partners that actually apply may process information under their service terms and privacy policies. Google’s public terms for a South Korean billing country identify Google Asia Pacific Pte. Ltd. as the contracting entity; Google’s privacy policy describes services of Google LLC and relevant affiliates. A contracting entity, its processing role and actual storage locations are not assumed to be the same thing.

The test build has no third-party mediation SDK and requests only Google sample ads. This does not guarantee that no other Google service providers or advertising-related parties ever process information. Where a consent message is provided, its partner information and those partners’ privacy policies can also be consulted.

Partner configuration may change with account settings and service scope, including automatic updates. A selected account list or a complete reference directory is not proof that every listed party actually received information. This development policy does not authorize production monetized advertising before the applicable scope and required consent have been established.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device game saves are retained for gameplay and maintaining progress/settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling through Android may delete them and cause loss of progress, currency and settings. Menus and backup behavior vary by device; OS backup/transfer data and external-service data are not guaranteed to be deleted simultaneously.

Google SDK/service retention varies by information type, purpose, account/service settings, security/fraud prevention and legal obligations. Some information is managed through deletion requests or account controls; some expires or is anonymized after specified periods, or is kept longer for limited reasons. There is no single fixed retention period asserted for all SDK data. Ad-report retention is not presented as the retention period of individual SDK-collected information.

Support messages and attachments held by the operator are deleted within 90 days after inquiry completion. Ongoing inquiries and legally required records are assessed individually, and unnecessary/sensitive information is minimized earlier. Independent security, backup or other processing by Google/GitHub is not guaranteed to be erased on the same deadline.

9. Rights and safeguards

You or a legally authorized guardian may send access, correction, deletion, suspension, consent-withdrawal or other requests under applicable law to the support email. Only minimum necessary verification will be requested. Do not send unsolicited passwords, verification codes or unnecessary identity-document copies.

The developer does not operate an administrative server that remotely reads or deletes on-device game saves. You may need to manage device-held information in device settings. Provider procedures also apply to information held directly by external providers. Uninstalling, changing consent or changing one Google account setting does not guarantee immediate deletion of every related record.

The app uses Android app-storage access restrictions. Save checksums detect corruption; they are not personal-data encryption. Access is limited to what is needed and unnecessary inquiry copies are minimized, but absolute security is not guaranteed.

10. Test instructions and policy updates

The current development build does not yet implement an always-accessible general policy/support URL link inside the app. Before subsequent tests, the operator will separately provide the published policy URL so reviewers can read it before participating. In-app access is a follow-up implementation and verification item. The UMP privacy-options button is not represented as the general policy link.

If features, SDKs, purposes, partners, operating audience or retention criteria change, the policy and effective date are updated and separate notice/consent procedures are used where required. Publishing this development/private-test policy does not start a child-inclusive public service or production monetized advertising.
