1. Scope

This policy describes on-device game saves, optional Google Play Games ranking, advertising and consent, and support inquiries for the SHIFTNAKE Android build named above, provided by Hanmin Kim. There is no in-app age check. The app does not ask for, read, or store an age. It does not set a child-directed flag (TFCD), an under-age-of-consent flag (TFUA), or an age-restricted treatment, and it does not force non-personalized ads as the only mode. The maximum ad content rating is T. SHIFTNAKE is not directed to children under 13; its Google Play target audience is ages 13 and over.

Visits to the support website and support email processing are explained separately in the website and inquiry privacy notice. This document concerns the Android app and is distinct from the website notice.

2. On-device game saves

The following information is stored in an app-private on-device database to provide gameplay and keep progress:

  • Progression and wallet
  • Achievements
  • Best scores
  • Selected skin, game mode, and difficulty
  • Sound and music on/off, volumes, language, one-hand mode, and graphics quality
  • Ad-display record: game-over count, whether an interstitial is due, and the 60-second cooldown after a rewarded ad
  • Daily reward records, stored only on the device: the daily fruit date (shiftnake-daily-fruit-date-v1), the daily shield date (shiftnake-daily-shield-date-v1), and the daily magnet date, count, and waiting flag (shiftnake-daily-magnet-v1)
  • Onboarding completed
  • A one-time local receipt for a tester fruit grant of 50,000 fruit

The last music track id is kept only for that session and is not part of this database. Music and sound-effect files are included in the app and are not uploaded.

There is no developer-operated account, login, or remote save server, and the app’s code does not upload these saves. There is no analytics or crash SDK. Google SDK communications, consent state stored by the UMP SDK, and operating-system backup are separate from this database.

Android backup is allowed. Depending on the device, operating-system settings, and backup service, app data may be copied by Android backup or device-to-device transfer. This is not an account-based cloud save run by the developer. An old file named shiftnake_age_band is excluded from that backup and transfer. The app does not read or write it.

3. Information not entered directly

The app does not require registration or entry of a name, phone number, government ID number, date of birth, or age. It does not request the camera, microphone, location, or contacts.

There is no in-app age check. Nothing in the app reads or writes an age.

Permissions declared for the app are INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, and com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID. Libraries add WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, and DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION. The build does not include ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, or ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS.

Ordinary online-status checking reads Android network-connectivity state, including internet, validated, and captive-portal flags. The app does not send a separate HTTP connectivity probe or upload a connection history to a developer server. Operating-system and external-SDK communications are separate.

4. Google advertising and consent SDKs and communications

This build includes Google Mobile Ads SDK (play-services-ads) 25.5.0, User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0, and Play Games Services (play-services-games-v2) 22.1.0. It does not include a mediation SDK, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or another analytics or crash SDK. Google measurement libraries that ship with the Ads SDK are included, and app-measurement initialization is delayed until the Ads SDK is initialized. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may use the Android advertising ID under the AD_ID permission and the device settings. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. When ads are requested and shown, Google may collect or receive information such as the advertising ID and other device or app identifiers, the IP address (which may be used to estimate an approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and other app activity, and diagnostic and performance information about the SDK. Categories that SDK may process are described in Google’s data disclosure linked at the end of this policy. See also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google AdMob.

This build uses the operator’s live Google AdMob ad units (one rewarded unit and one interstitial unit), so real ads from Google and its ad-technology partners are shown. Every ad is full screen. There are no banner ads. A reward is granted only after it is earned and the ad is closed. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one:

  • Continue after game over, on the result screen, once per run
  • 2× fruit on the result screen after a death or a win, once per run, and only when that run’s fruit is greater than zero
  • Daily fruit +100 in the shop, once per device-local day
  • Shield +1 for the day, from the shop shield card, once per day. That shield is applied on SHIFT and CLAIM runs that day
  • Magnet for one run, from the shop magnet card, up to 3 times per day, with at most one run waiting. The card is hidden once the magnet is owned
  • A 24-hour skin trial from the shop skin view. Only one trial is active at a time. Celestial skins need two ads

An interstitial can be shown when a new run starts (START or RETRY). It becomes due after 3 finished game overs. It is not shown within 60 seconds of a completed rewarded ad, and it is skipped if it was not preloaded.

Google’s automatic Mobile Ads initializer and Play Games initializer are not used. At launch the app makes a read-only UMP consent-information request. That request does not show a form and does not start the Ads SDK. It only supplies the consent status shown in Settings. If UMP reports that consent is required, the consent form is loaded and shown at the first START of a new run or at the first rewarded ad. The app then checks whether UMP allows an ad request. Only in that case does it initialize Mobile Ads, in a separate process, with maximum ad content rating T, and load an ad. The app does not set non-personalized ads as the only mode, and it does not set TFCD, TFUA, or an age-restricted treatment. The app does not turn ads off by age or region: whenever Google’s UMP indicates that ads can be requested (including regions where no consent is required), ads are requested the same way for every user. In the EEA/UK, ads may still be requested after you decline consent, in which case Google serves non-personalized or limited ads according to your choices. If the consent check cannot be completed (for example, a network error) or no ad is available, the game continues without an ad, and rewards are granted only after an ad is watched to the end and closed. The app does not add its own region rules. Google UMP decides whether a message is needed, including EEA and UK messages and US state messages.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

There is no in-app age check and no control that sets an age. The maximum ad content rating is T. Child-directed (TFCD), under-age-of-consent (TFUA), and age-restricted treatment flags are not set. The app does not force non-personalized ads as the only mode. These statements do not mean that the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes no information.

The consent form is not shown at launch. When Google UMP requires a choice, it is shown at the first START or the first rewarded ad. The app does not turn ads off by age or region: whenever Google’s UMP indicates that ads can be requested (including regions where no consent is required), ads are requested the same way for every user. In the EEA/UK, ads may still be requested after you decline consent, in which case Google serves non-personalized or limited ads according to your choices. If the consent check cannot be completed (for example, a network error) or no ad is available, the game continues without an ad, and rewards are granted only after an ad is watched to the end and closed.

When UMP reports that privacy options are required, “Privacy options” in Settings opens that form so you can change or withdraw the choice. Korean and English privacy-policy links in Settings open in an external browser and do not depend on an advertising-consent choice. If the consent status could not be confirmed, “Retry with current choice” in Settings checks it again. Changing a choice does not, by itself, delete information Google already holds.

Support requests that need personal information about a user under 14 should come through a parent or guardian. Do not send unnecessary child names, exact birth dates, photos, or locations. If unnecessary child information is identified, minimal handling and prompt minimization or deletion take priority.

OS backup or transfer copies and external-service data are not guaranteed to be deleted at the same time.

12. Optional Google Play Games ranking

Ranking is optional and uses Google Play Games Services. The Play Games SDK is not initialized at launch. It is initialized when the ranking screen is opened or when a SHIFT or CLAIM run finishes. The app first checks whether Play Games is already authenticated. Depending on the Play Games automatic sign-in setting, that check can connect without a tap. Interactive sign-in runs only from the sign-in button on the ranking screen. Ordinary play remains available without sign-in. Ranking can be limited by network connectivity and Google service availability.

When a SHIFT or CLAIM run ends, and the run is not practice or a tutorial, and the player is already signed in, the app sends that run’s mode, difficulty, and score to one of six leaderboards: SHIFT or CLAIM, each with easy, normal, and crazy. CLASSIC runs are not submitted. Fruit and purchases are not submitted. The submission queue is kept in memory only. The app’s Play Games log lines contain mode, difficulty, and result codes, and those lines are not uploaded to a developer server.

For the signed-in player, the app receives the player ID, display name, and icon URI. From public leaderboards it receives other players’ player IDs, display names, ranks, and scores. The ranking screen shows names and does not display icon URLs. Only the public collection is read. The feature does not request an email address. The developer does not operate a ranking server.

During sign-in, submission, and retrieval, Google may process information under its own policies. Google may process information on servers worldwide, and Play Games account and ranking information may be processed outside South Korea. Google’s policies and account procedures also govern its processing, retention, international processing, and deletion. Uninstalling the app or clearing its on-device saves does not guarantee deletion of Google-held leaderboard records. Play Games account and profile settings are where the sign-in account and automatic sign-in choices are managed.
