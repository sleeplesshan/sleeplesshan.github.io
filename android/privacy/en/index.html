SHIFTNAKE Android Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026 (from publication) · Previous version: effective November 12, 2026

  • App: SHIFTNAKE Android (com.sleeplesshan.shiftnake)
  • Applies to: SHIFTNAKE Android 1.0.0 and later. The Play audience is ages 13 and older (13–15, 16–17, 18+). The build that no longer asks for age and uses the same ad request for every user is versionCode 90 (runtime v158).
  • Operator: Hanmin Kim, an individual developer based in South Korea
  • Privacy contact: hankim+shiftnake@mail.grokbot.com

1. Scope

This policy describes game saves, optional Google Play Games ranking, advertising and privacy choices, and support inquiries for SHIFTNAKE Android, provided by Hanmin Kim. The app’s target audience is users aged 13 and older; it is not directed to children under 13. The app does not ask for age and stores no age data. Every user follows the same ad request. A UMP consent check runs first, and the ad SDK is initialized and ads are loaded only if UMP allows ad requests. Whether ads are personalized follows UMP consent; non-personalized ads are not forced. The maximum ad content rating is T for everyone.

Visits to the support website and support email processing are explained separately in the website and inquiry privacy notice. This document concerns the Android app and is distinct from the website notice.

2. On-device game saves

The following information is stored in the device’s app storage to provide gameplay and maintain progress:

  • In-game currency, progress and best records, unlocks and owned/equipped skins
  • Selected game mode, music, sound effects, volumes, track and language
  • One-handed input, rendering quality and other display/control settings
  • Local records needed for deaths, continuing a run and ad timing
  • Rewarded-ad records: whether the current run’s 2x fruit bonus was used, skin-trial progress (for example 1 of 2 ads) and trial start/end times, and the date a shop reward of 100 fruit was claimed (once per day, on-device key shiftnake-daily-fruit-date-v1)

Current native persistence uses app-private SQLite and other on-device storage paths. There is no developer-operated game account, login or remote save server, and no implemented feature uploading these game saves to a developer server. Google SDK communications and operating-system backup are separate paths.

The current Android configuration permits system backup. Depending on the device, operating-system settings and backup service, app data may be backed up or transferred to and restored on another device. This does not mean that the app provides its own account-based cloud-save feature, nor guarantee that no information ever leaves the device.

3. Information not entered directly

The app does not require registration or entry of a name, phone number, government ID number, date of birth, or age group. Features requiring contacts, camera, microphone or precise location are not currently implemented.

The Play audience is ages 13 and older (13–15, 16–17, 18+). The app does not ask for age and does not store new age data. A previously saved age value (shiftnake_age_band) may remain on the device, but this version never reads it and does not change or delete it. That value stays excluded from Android backup and device-to-device transfer. Game-save formats are unchanged. UMP-managed consent state is stored separately.

4. Google advertising and consent SDKs and communications

The current build includes Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. No separate Firebase Analytics or third-party mediation SDK has been added. Builds configured for test ads use Google-provided sample rewarded and interstitial ad units; builds configured for production ads use the operator’s registered AdMob ad units. The advertising and consent SDK communications and privacy-choice rules below also apply when sample ads are used. After the UMP consent check, ads may be requested in advance so they are ready to display. Rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one: (1) an ad-rewarded continue, (2) a 2x fruit bonus on the result screen (once per run), (3) a 24-hour trial of a locked skin (Celestial-tier skins need two ads), or (4) 100 fruit from the shop (once per day). A reward is granted only after the ad is watched to completion and closed. An interstitial may be shown on a retry after multiple deaths, and not within 60 seconds after a rewarded ad. Availability depends on privacy choices, connectivity, Google ad serving and app approval, among other factors.

The app blocks automatic ad-SDK initialization. For every user, the UMP consent check runs before the Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized, and ad initialization and loading proceed only if UMP allows ad requests. UMP consent-information requests and preparation/display of a required form may communicate before an ad is loaded. The app code checks UMP’s permission to request ads and SDK readiness before requesting an ad and does not bypass an error or closed gate. This is not an enumeration of every internal SDK or operating-system action, or a guarantee of no communications before a particular point.

Google discloses that its advertising SDK automatically collects and shares the following data categories for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This app applies feature and privacy-choice settings:

  • IP address and approximate location that may be inferred from it
  • App launches, taps, ad/video views and other usage/interactions
  • App/SDK performance, launch time, hangs, energy usage and diagnostics
  • Device-, app- or account-related identifiers, including the Android advertising ID (AD_ID) where available and permitted by your device settings, and App Set ID
  • Related information needed to check consent and provide messages and privacy-choice management

From code15 the app declares the Android advertising-ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) so that the Google Mobile Ads SDK can use the advertising ID for ad delivery and measurement, subject to your consent choices and device settings. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. Certain Privacy Sandbox (AdServices) permissions remain removed. Google describes TLS protection for advertising-SDK data in transit; this is not a guarantee that all stored data is independently encrypted or absolutely secure.

Ordinary online-status checking reads Android network-connectivity and validation state. The app’s network adapter does not implement separate HTTP connectivity probes or upload a connection history to a developer server. Operating-system and external-SDK communications are separate.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Every user follows the same ad request. The app does not block ads by age, and it does not set a child-directed or under-age-of-consent tag or a teen age treatment. The maximum ad content rating is T for everyone. Whether ads are personalized follows UMP consent, and non-personalized ads are not forced. These settings do not mean that no personal information is collected, and they are not the same as a sale/sharing opt-out signal.

The app is not directed to children under 13. It does not provide a separate guardian-verification function and does not assume a parent has consented to advertising for a child. Support requests needing personal information about a user under 14 should come through a parent or guardian. Do not send unnecessary child names, exact birth dates, photos or locations. If unnecessary child information is identified, minimal handling and prompt minimization/deletion take priority.

There is no menu to choose or change an age. When UMP reports that privacy options are required and a form is available, “Privacy options” in Settings opens the form so you can change or withdraw consent. Availability depends on region, SDK status, message availability and other conditions.

Separate Korean and English privacy-policy links open in an external browser without requiring an advertising-consent choice. An ad/consent-check failure is not intended to indefinitely block an ordinary online new game. Changing a consent choice does not delete all information already held by Google or partners; deletion and rights requests may involve separate procedures.

8. Retention and deletion

On-device game saves are retained for gameplay and maintaining progress/settings. Clearing app data or uninstalling through Android may delete them and cause loss of progress, currency and settings. Menus and backup behavior vary by device; OS backup/transfer data and external-service data are not guaranteed to be deleted simultaneously.

Google SDK/service retention varies by information type, purpose, account/service settings, security/fraud prevention and legal obligations. Some information is managed through deletion requests or account controls; some expires or is anonymized after specified periods, or is kept longer for limited reasons. There is no single fixed retention period asserted for all SDK data. Ad-report retention is not presented as the retention period of individual SDK-collected information.

Support messages and attachments held by the operator are deleted within 90 days after inquiry completion. Ongoing inquiries and legally required records are assessed individually, and unnecessary/sensitive information is minimized earlier. Independent security, backup or other processing by the mailbox provider or GitHub is not guaranteed to be erased on the same deadline.

9. Rights and safeguards

You or a legally authorized guardian may send access, correction, deletion, suspension, consent-withdrawal or other requests under applicable law to the support email. Only minimum necessary verification will be requested. Do not send unsolicited passwords, verification codes or unnecessary identity-document copies.

The developer does not operate an administrative server that remotely reads or deletes on-device game saves. You may need to manage device-held information in device settings. Provider procedures also apply to information held directly by external providers. Uninstalling, changing consent or changing one Google account setting does not guarantee immediate deletion of every related record.

The app uses Android app-storage access restrictions. Save checksums detect corruption; they are not personal-data encryption. Access is limited to what is needed and unnecessary inquiry copies are minimized, but absolute security is not guaranteed.

12. Optional Google Play Games ranking

Ranking is an optional feature used by connecting to Google Play Games. Existing Play Games automatic sign-in settings may connect the app without another tap on its sign-in button. While connected, scores from games that have actually finished and been finalized may be submitted automatically without opening the ranking screen or pressing a submit button each time. Ordinary gameplay remains available without sign-in. Network connectivity and Google service availability can affect ranking.

The feature receives the Play Games player identifier, gamer name and any supplied profile-image URL to display your account and records, and submits the score of an actually finished and finalized game to its mode-specific Google Play Games Services leaderboard. To display weekly/all-time records and ranks, it also receives other public participants’ player identifiers, gamer names, any supplied profile-image URLs, scores and ranks. Fruit currency, skin purchases and past on-device best scores are not submitted as leaderboard scores. The feature does not request your email address; the developer does not operate a separate membership-account or ranking server.

Public-list visibility follows your Google Play Games profile and gameplay-sharing settings. The app does not make your profile public or enable email discoverability. During sign-in, submission and retrieval, Google may process information under its own policies for service delivery, account management, security and fraud prevention. Google may process information on servers worldwide, and Play Games account/ranking information may be processed outside South Korea. Google’s policies and account procedures also govern its processing, retention, international processing and deletion. Uninstalling the app or clearing its on-device saves does not guarantee deletion of Google-held leaderboard records.

Google’s Play Games account/profile settings let you manage the sign-in account and automatic sign-in choices for games. Use profile, game-data and privacy controls for visibility and data-deletion procedures. The app does not check age, and the Play Games connection is a separate setting. The app does not verify Play Games identity or guardian consent, and it does not turn off the Play Games connection.
